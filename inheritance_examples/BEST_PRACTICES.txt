Best Practices & Notes
----------------------
1. Favor composition over inheritance where appropriate.
2. Use 'is-a' relationship for inheritance (e.g., Dog is-a Animal).
3. Mark methods final if they should not be overridden.
4. Use @Override annotation when overriding methods.
5. Keep fields private/protected and provide accessors if needed.
6. Avoid calling overridable methods from constructors.
7. Prefer interfaces for multiple-inheritance-like behavior.
8. Test interactions between superclass and subclasses.
